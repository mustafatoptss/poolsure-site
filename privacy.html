English

Short version: your pool data never leaves your phone. PoolSure has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Pool details, test results, products and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them. Backups you export are files you control.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is sold through the App Store or Google Play. To confirm it, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and a random, anonymous app identifier. No name, email or other contact detail is sent. This data is used only to unlock Pro and restore it on your devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Children

PoolSure is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: contact support.
